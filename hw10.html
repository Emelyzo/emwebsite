Motherhood in Crisis, tells different stories of women, all dealing with troubles in motherhood. Whether they suffered from abuse or the failure of the system they live in, they found a community of people to help them. Doctors who support the well-being of the child and mother. All of these stories are based on true accounts from women who have endured such pain but eventually found a way out of it. The use of drawing and graphic designs, the story came out like a comic book, really bringing the stories to life for us. The dialogue went with each of the situations that happened in the stories. You can feel the fear and the pain that the mother went through. It's like their pain is reflecting out of the page and it did make me feel sad about their situation. The emotions that were shown with the designs of the pictures really brought everything to life, and we could almost feel everything they had been through, or just feel saddened and even fearful for them
