Motherhood In Crisis 🤰🏽💪🏾


Motherhood in Crisis, tells different stories of women, all dealing with troubles in motherhood. Whether they suffered from abuse or the failure of the system they live in, they found a community of people to help them. Doctors who support the well-being of the child and mother. All of these stories are based on true accounts from women who have endured such pain but eventually found a way out of it.

The use of drawing and graphic designs, the story came out like a comic book, really bringing the stories to life for us. The dialogue went with each of the situations that happened in the stories. You can feel the fear and the pain that the mother went through. It's like their pain is reflecting out of the page and it did make me feel sad about their situation. The emotions that were shown with the designs of the pictures really brought everything to life, and we could almost feel everything they had been through, or just feel saddened and even fearful for them

The flow of the scenes summarizes the majority of things that happen in a short way. The designs show innocence and trouble, the people surrounding the storyline. When something scary or horrible is about to happen, the person who represents evil is shown with a dark shadow. The designs of facial features really just capture what these women and people do in real life.

I think one of the stories that stuck with me was “My Stolen Future” because she was just a young girl when she was assaulted by someone she trusted. And to become pregnant after doing something against her will. It wasn’t her fault, and in society, there are many stories where women have overcome their assaults and become activists against these horrific acts, speaking up for young girls and women who have probably never gotten justice. Here we see that in the end, she got her justice and gained a support system that would help her continue in life. She picked herself up from where she had fallen and made it her goal to come out of it, not letting her past define who she would become.

I guess I just feel sad that the future she might have wanted was now 100 miles out of her reach. However she did have a support system that helped her with her situation, whether it was her friend or a grandmother, she had someone to depend on for the situation she was put in. Which is what was so inspiring, because when people who are in these situations often feel only, there will always be someone who will show up and be that person that you need to pull you out of that hole.
