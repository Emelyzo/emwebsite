AI-Slop

In “‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster” by Nesrine Malik discusses the dangers of AI and how it is being used in political propaganda to serve and support the political agendas of politicians.

AI has its benefits for society but it also has its negatives which could cause major problems for everyone. While it has made an impact in several ways such as medical and business-related, it also causes alarms for other fields of work that would most likely be at risk of taking jobs from actual humans. It doesn’t just affect work fields, but also the education of students. AI has become a way for students to take the easy way and to cheat on assignments and exams.
